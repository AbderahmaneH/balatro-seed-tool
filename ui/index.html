Your choices in this run

Each shop has an ordered list of actions: reroll, buy a voucher, open a pack, take a card, use a consumable, sell a joker. Order matters, because the game's randomness advances with every step: opening pack A before B, or buying a voucher before or after a reroll, gives different cards. Use the arrows on a step to reorder.
